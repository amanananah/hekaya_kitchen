# Privacy decisions

- Family content is private by default and is never used to train a model without separate, explicit consent.
- The recorder confirms the elder's consent before upload.
- Access is limited to invited family-circle members and checked on every media request.
- Recordings, transcripts, translations, attempts, and feedback are encrypted in transit and at rest.
- Notifications reveal no recipe, image, voice, or cultural-story content.
- Elders can correct generated text and edit or delete their recordings.
- Learners can delete their attempts. Lesson owners can remove feedback from the evolving lesson.
- The product records who confirmed each step and when; AI output is never labelled “family verified” on its own.
- A documented retention window and deletion-completion audit are required before launch.
- Logs exclude transcripts, signed media URLs, access tokens, and other family content.

The prototype contains only fictional sample family data. Its temporary recording URI and demo state are not a production privacy implementation.
