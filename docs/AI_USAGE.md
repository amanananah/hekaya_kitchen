# Human-led development and AI assistance record

This record provides the transparency required by the competition. Hekaya Kitchen's problem definition, family focus, cultural direction, feature priorities, safety boundaries, and final acceptance decisions came from the human team. AI tools supported implementation and drafting under that direction. They were not treated as the source of cultural knowledge or final product judgment.

Do not include secrets, personal family recordings, or private data in this record.

## 2026-09-03 — product reliability revision

**Human goal:** Refocus Hekaya Kitchen as an intergenerational cooking platform and remove unreliable technical claims.

**Human direction:** Keep transcription, translation, lesson structuring, story extraction, and clarification questions. Remove automatic measurement, quality evaluation, live action recognition, confidence percentages, and autonomous safety advice. Complete the elder-teaches → learner-practises → elder-responds loop and document architecture, privacy, tests, and responsibilities.

**Assistance used:** Codex helped draft implementation changes and documentation for the teacher-confirmation flow, attempt sharing, feedback, architecture, privacy, and testing evidence.

**Human verification required:** Product owner reviews cultural language and scope; technical lead verifies implementation claims; elder users validate the review flow; the team runs and records device tests.

## 2026-09-06 — identity, accessibility, and family workflow

**Human goal:** Rename the product to Hekaya Kitchen, keep cooking as the clear focus, replace presentation-like language with normal app language, and make the experience usable by any family teacher—not only a grandmother.

**Human decisions:** The team selected the name Hekaya Kitchen; requested Easy Mode; required large controls, minimal typing, Arabic and right-to-left support; rejected generic use of “Grandma”; and chose the relationship loop of teacher → learner attempt → teacher response.

**Assistance used:** Codex helped implement the renamed interface, Easy Mode, bilingual one-question review, Cook Together, attempt sharing, personalised teacher labels, and supporting repository documentation. TypeScript checks and browser-based visual reviews were used to catch implementation and layout issues.

**Outputs changed after human review:** “Grandma Mode” became “Easy Mode”; generic “Grandma” wording became “family cook,” “teacher,” or the selected person's name; promotional presentation copy was replaced with task-focused app language; unsupported confidence percentages and automatic cooking judgments were excluded.

**Human verification required:** The team must complete physical-device testing, verify Arabic with a fluent reviewer, validate cultural descriptions with participating family members, and approve the final competition demonstration.
